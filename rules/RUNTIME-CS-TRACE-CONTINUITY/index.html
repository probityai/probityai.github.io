RUNTIME-CS-TRACE-CONTINUITY

A multi-hop A2A delegation chain failed to preserve its context_id across hops — a hop dropped (set null) or forked the conversation context, severing the delegation lineage the Composition-Safety scope-attenuation check depends on. Flagged as a mesh-hygiene advisory distinct from a confirmed confused-deputy attack: a severed lineage may be a benign framework boundary (a background queue or fresh thread that did not propagate the trace) or may mask a self-escalating hop, so it is surfaced for investigation rather than alarmed as a confirmed exploit. The mesh observer reads only the context_id / reference_task_ids topology off the ExecutionResult chain.
