RUNTIME-CROSS-AGENT-TAINT-TAMPERED

An inbound cross-agent taint bundle was present on the A2A envelope but did not verify: forged or malformed (its MAC failed or the bundle was unparseable), attribution-forged (the declared sender did not match the verifying-key identity), or replayed (a valid blob lifted from another session or past its expiry). A present-but-invalid bundle is an active tamper of the value-taint wire, surfaced as a finding rather than silently dropped; an absent bundle maps to untainted with no finding. Carries the CWE-345 insufficient-verification-of-authenticity taxonomy (CWE-294 capture-replay for the replayed variant)
