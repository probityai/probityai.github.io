RUNTIME-CS-CYCLE

A multi-hop A2A delegation chain contains a directed cycle — a task transitively references itself, so subtasks refine or forward one another forever without producing a final result, exhausting scheduler capacity. This is the structural signature of the A2ASecBench Cycle-Overflow availability attack. Detected deterministically by the delegation-DAG topology analyzer off the task_id / reference_task_ids chain; no live LLM.
