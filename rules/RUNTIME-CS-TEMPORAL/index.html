RUNTIME-CS-TEMPORAL

A multi-hop A2A delegation chain exercised its root grant after the grant's effective TTL had elapsed in transit: a provable expired-grant replay (temporal TOCTOU). The declared-TTL lattice check compares TTL values, so a token cached past its window verifies green there; the observed-timestamp check catches it. Skew-aware: an elapsed time inside the observed clock-skew band, or a downstream hop stamped before the root, is INDETERMINATE and surfaced as a per-check notApplicable rather than a false escalation. The mesh observer reads only the hop timestamps and the root-grant TTL off the chain; no live LLM.
