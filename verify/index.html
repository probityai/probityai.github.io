Check a record in your browser

Paste an evaluation-claim record (bare or as an in-toto Statement), the acceptance rule you hold, and the case sets you accept. The reader from agent-evidence-vectors judges it here, in your browser: the wheel is fetched from PyPI, its SHA-256 and those of the Pyodide runtime files are checked against digests pinned in this page, and nothing you paste leaves the page.

The rule is yours, never the producer's: it pins the profile and the case-set digests you accept, so a record built from a reduced case set, or from a table with one expectation flipped, is refused. The reader does not re-run the cases or check a signature over the record.


  
Package agent-evidence-vectors 0.37.0. To run the same check offline:
    pip install agent-evidence-vectors==0.37.0, then
    agent-evidence-vectors claim verify --record R --rule RULE --case-set C.
