Aggregate accounting, contract v1

This URI identifies version 1 of the aggregate-accounting record: the accounting decisions taken against one budget shared by a principal and the delegation subtrees under it. A record written against it carries:

"contractId": "https://probityai.github.io/agent-evidence-observer/contract/aggregate-accounting/v1"

The reference cases and the expected decision for each are in vectors-aggregate-accounting in the agent-evidence-vectors repository. A reader refuses a record that omits this identifier or names another version.
