Dispatch fence, contract v1

This URI identifies version 1 of the dispatch-fence record: one exclusive dispatch: the policy check over shared state, the fence taken to dispatch, the effect and the commit of its result. A record written against it carries:

"contractId": "https://probityai.github.io/agent-evidence-observer/contract/dispatch-fence/v1"

The reference cases and the expected decision for each are in vectors-dispatch-fence in the agent-evidence-vectors repository. A reader refuses a record that omits this identifier or names another version.
